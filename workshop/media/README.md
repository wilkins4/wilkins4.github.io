## Export rules

- Use original Workshop photographs, not manufacturer or marketplace images.
- Export to sRGB WebP.
- Keep the subject sharp and the background controlled.
- Remove personal documents, addresses, labels, and unrelated family items.
- Do not hide normal wear that is relevant to an honest recommendation.
- Keep a high-resolution original outside the website repository.
- Aim for less than 300 KB per card image and less than 500 KB for wide guide images.
- Verify the drafted alt text in the private `/workshop/preview/` dashboard after each image is added.
