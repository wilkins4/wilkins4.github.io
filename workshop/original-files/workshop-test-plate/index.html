What it tests

Five comparison groups on one plate

  • Raised line widths from 0.20 to 1.00 mm.
  • Recessed groove widths from 0.20 to 1.00 mm.
  • Square post and recess sizes from 0.50 to 1.50 mm.
  • Relief heights of 0.10, 0.40, and 0.80 mm.
  • Parallel, grid, rivet, and checker texture fields.

Suggested first print

Keep the comparison controlled

  1. Confirm a 60 × 40 mm import.
  2. Print feature side up.
  3. Use 0.05 mm layers and an already functional resin profile.
  4. Keep washing and curing identical between comparisons.
  5. Change one setting at a time and record it in the included CSV.
