Core method

Keep the master mix separate from precision work

The sprue glue stays in a sealed master jar. Its worn lid brush remains useful for broad application, but precision work starts by moving a small amount to a disposable work surface. That prevents repeated contamination and avoids forcing one applicator to handle every consistency and seam.

Precision applicators

  • Blunt dispensing needle for small controlled deposits.
  • Bamboo detail swab for mixing and transferring thicker material.
  • Old brush only when bristle control is actually useful.

Known limitation

A syringe can work for one session, but sprue glue cures inside the barrel. It should be treated as disposable or cleaned immediately, not used as a storage container.

Tool selection

Use the smallest tool that can place the required amount

  • Lid brush: large gaps, broad seams, and rough internal surfaces.
  • Blunt needle: controlled beads and narrow visible seams.
  • Bamboo swab: mixing, texture adjustment, and disposable placement.
  • Syringe: short-session transfer when volume control matters.
  • Sanding sticks and files: final leveling only after a full cure.

Apply thin layers and allow solvent time to work. Packing a deep gap in one pass increases shrinkage, surface damage, and cleanup time.

i

Plastic only

Sprue glue is a solvent-and-plastic filler for compatible polystyrene. Resin, metal, mixed-material joints, and structural repairs need a different adhesive or filler.
