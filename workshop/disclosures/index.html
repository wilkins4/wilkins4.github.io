Disclosures, licensing, and site policies

The Workshop foundation treats recommendation status, affiliate relationships, intellectual property, privacy, and external checkout as product features rather than footer afterthoughts.

Affiliate disclosure

Some future links may be affiliate links. When a link can generate a commission, the related card or recommendation will display a visible paid-link label. A commission does not increase the visitor's purchase price.

No affiliate links are active in this foundation preview. Current outbound product links point to official manufacturer or marketplace pages for reference.

Recommendation standard

  • Personally used means the item is part of the Workshop setup.
  • Tested means there is enough direct use to document practical strengths and limitations.
  • Directory seed means the source is organized for discovery, not personally endorsed.
  • Paid link means a financial relationship exists and is disclosed near the recommendation.

External checkout

Wilkinson Workshop does not currently collect payment information, process orders, deliver paid files, or store customer accounts. Product buttons will route to external marketplaces that publish their own payment, delivery, refund, and privacy terms.

Original file status

Original products are labeled by release stage. Concept, design queue, prototype, testing, and ready statuses are not interchangeable. A preview card is not a promise that a product is immediately available.

Licensing and intellectual property

Original Workshop files use original geometry and descriptive hobby themes. The product plan excludes scanned miniatures, extracted game assets, protected faction logos, and near-identical recreations of third-party products.

Third-party STL files are never redistributed. Physical prints based on third-party files require documented commercial permission for the specific creator, product, and license period.

Privacy and analytics

The static Workshop foundation does not create user accounts or store payment data. It contains an analytics-ready outbound-click event, but only sends data to Google Analytics when an existing gtag implementation is present on the page.

The event records the catalog entry, entry type, destination, and source path. It does not intentionally include email addresses, payment details, or message content.
