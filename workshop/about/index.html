About the project

A practical front door for the whole hobby workflow

Wilkinson Workshop is being built as one place to organize original designs, real workbench equipment, useful creators, and trusted suppliers without pretending the first version needs a full commerce platform.

Original products first

Digital files and small physical products provide the highest control over design, documentation, pricing, and customer support.

Recommendations earn trust

Personally used, tested, directory-only, and paid-link statuses are separate fields instead of vague marketing language.

External services handle sales

Established marketplaces manage payment collection and file delivery while this site remains the permanent brand and product-information layer.
